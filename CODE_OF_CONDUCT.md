# Code of conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [Português (Brasil)](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pt-BR.md)

We want a welcoming, constructive community for readers and contributors of all experience levels. Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make room for different perspectives. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

Maintain appropriate boundaries when working with reading data: do not post real libraries, device files, credentials, or private reading histories without the owner's permission. Use fictional examples when reporting bugs or contributing tests.

## Reporting and enforcement

For security concerns, use the private reporting guidance in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For a conduct concern, ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without including personal details in a public issue. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation when needed. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.

This policy draws on the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
